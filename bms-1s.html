LiGen BMS - 1S

Overview: The LiGen BMS - 1S is a 1s Battery Management System designed for LFP (Lithium Iron Phosphate) battery cells. This patented BMS provides intelligent monitoring, protection, and balancing for your battery pack, ensuring optimal performance, safety, and longevity.

Key Features: Advanced protection against over-charge, over-discharge, over-current, short-circuit, and temperature extremes. Passive cell balancing ensures uniform cell voltage. Low self-consumption with working current ≤ 20 μA and sleeping current ≤ 10 μA. Wide operating temperature range from -30°C to 85°C.

Applications: Ideal for electric vehicles, solar energy storage systems, home backup power units, portable power modules, and industrial battery packs. The 1S configuration provides 3.65 V charging voltage with 1-100 A continuous discharge current capability.
