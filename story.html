Our Story

Ligen Power® is a leading manufacturer and supplier of high-quality lithium battery solutions. With years of experience in the energy storage industry, we specialize in designing, developing, and producing advanced lithium-ion batteries for various applications including consumer electronics, electric vehicles, energy storage systems, and industrial equipment.

Our commitment to innovation, quality, and sustainability has positioned us as a trusted partner for businesses worldwide. We combine cutting-edge technology with rigorous testing protocols to ensure our batteries deliver exceptional performance, safety, and longevity.
