Event Details

Date: Recent
Location: Bharat Mandapam, New Delhi

The Bihar @ 2047 Vision Conclave, a pivotal event under the "Let's Inspire Bihar" initiative, recently concluded at Bharat Mandapam in New Delhi. This gathering united over 2,000 entrepreneurs, startups, and thought leaders from Bihar and its diaspora, all dedicated to envisioning a prosperous future for the state.

Vision 2047 Document Unveiled

A highlight of the conclave was the unveiling of the Vision 2047 Document, which outlines actionable strategies across 16 sectors, including education, entrepreneurship, health, agriculture, environment conservation, and tourism. This comprehensive plan aims to transform Bihar into a developed state by 2047, ensuring that residents no longer need to migrate for education, employment, or healthcare.

Ligen Power® as Supporting Partner

Ligen Power®, introduced as Amossys Portable Power with the brand Ligen Power®, proudly participated as a supporting partner in this transformative event. Our commitment to innovation and sustainable energy solutions aligns seamlessly with the conclave's emphasis on entrepreneurship and technological advancement.

Distinguished Guests and Leadership

Distinguished guests, including MP Manoj Tiwari and MLA Shreyasi Singh, praised the campaign's efforts and encouraged mass participation to realize Bihar's potential. The event concluded with attendees chanting, "I will transform Bihar," reaffirming their commitment to the campaign's vision.
