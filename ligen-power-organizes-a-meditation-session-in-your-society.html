Procedure: How to Meditate

Meditation doesn't demand hours—just a few minutes of mindful breathing can transform your day.

Follow these simple steps:

  1. Sit comfortably
  2. Cross your legs and fingers
  3. Close your eyes gently
  4. Focus on your natural breath – inhale and exhale
  5. Let go of thoughts and keep your mind anchored to your breath

This is known as Ana Pana Sati – a powerful breathing observation technique that leads to deep awareness and inner stillness.
