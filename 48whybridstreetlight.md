Hybrid solar lights are an innovative street lighting solution that combines solar energy with AC power. These lights feature solar panels that charge a battery during the day, storing energy for use at night to power efficient LED lights. Additionally, they are connected to the electrical grid, providing a backup power source when the battery is depleted. This dual energy source ensures that the lights remain operational even on cloudy days or in areas with limited sunlight. By leveraging both solar and AC power, hybrid solar lights offer a reliable and energy-efficient lighting option that helps lower energy costs and reduce dependence on conventional power sources.
