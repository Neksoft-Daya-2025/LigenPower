Introducing the Ligen Power® Patented Battery Management System (BMS)

Every great power solution needs a smart, reliable, and unique core. Our Patented Battery Management System (BMS) is precisely that—the "brain" of your battery, meticulously engineered to ensure its safety, optimize its performance, and extend its life. It's more than just a circuit; it's the guardian of your power system, providing real-time data and protection based on proprietary technology that no one else can offer.

Our patented BMS product line is designed to work with various lithium-ion battery chemistries, including LFP, NMC, and LCO. Whether you're building a solution for an electric vehicle, a solar energy storage system, or a home backup power unit, our BMS provides the intelligent control you need to protect your investment and ensure dependable power with technology you can truly trust.

Key Features & Benefits

Patented Safety & Protection

Our BMS actively monitors and controls key metrics like cell temperature, overcharge, and over-discharge, preventing potential damage with our unique and legally protected technology.

Optimized Performance

Features like passive cell balancing ensure that every cell within the battery pack works uniformly, maximizing efficiency and providing a more consistent power output.

Increased Longevity

By preventing common issues like over-discharging and maintaining optimal cell health, our patented BMS helps to significantly extend the lifespan of your battery.

Comprehensive Communication

Our systems support a variety of communication protocols (including BLE, WiFi, and CAN) to provide real-time monitoring and data analytics, allowing you to manage and optimize your power solution with ease.
