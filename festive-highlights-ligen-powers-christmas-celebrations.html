Festive Highlights: Ligen Power®'s Christmas Celebrations

December 2024 Bangalore, Patna, Jamshedpur
Back to News & Events

Event Details

Date: December 2024
Location: Bangalore, Patna, Jamshedpur

Christmas at Ligen Power® is more than just a holiday; it's a celebration of togetherness, creativity, and gratitude that resonates across all our locations. This year, the festive spirit lit up our head office in Bangalore, the manufacturing unit at IIT Incubation Centre in Patna, and the sales region in Jamshedpur. Let's take a closer look at the festivities that made this Christmas truly magical.

Bangalore: A Symphony of Office Festivities

At the Ligen Power® head office in Bangalore, Christmas began with a vibrant office decoration contest. Employees adorned the workspace with sparkling fairy lights, handcrafted ornaments, and a grand Christmas tree that stood as the centerpiece. The highlight of the day was a Secret Santa exchange, where heartfelt gifts brought smiles and laughter to everyone. The management team surprised employees with personalized gift hampers, emphasizing appreciation and camaraderie.

The celebration culminated with a sumptuous Christmas feast featuring an array of dishes and desserts that reflected both traditional and modern tastes. A live band performance added an extra touch of charm, turning the office into a hub of music and merriment.

Patna: Innovation Meets Festivity at IIT Incubation Centre

At the manufacturing unit located at the prestigious IIT Incubation Centre in Patna, the Christmas spirit took on a unique flavor. Engineers and staff gathered for a day filled with innovation-themed games and activities, blending the festive mood with Ligen Power®'s ethos of technological advancement.

The unit's highlight was a "Light Up the Tree" contest, where teams created eco-friendly, battery-powered Christmas lights. This activity not only showcased the creativity of the team but also reflected Ligen Power®'s commitment to sustainability. The day ended with a special recognition ceremony, where team members' contributions throughout the year were acknowledged, making the celebration both joyous and meaningful.

Jamshedpur: Spreading Smiles Through Community Outreach

The sales team in Jamshedpur celebrated Christmas by organizing a community outreach program. Team members distributed gifts and essential supplies to underprivileged children in the area, embodying the true spirit of giving. The initiative was followed by a cozy gathering where team members shared their year-end reflections and set goals for the upcoming year.

A mini Christmas carnival was also set up at the regional office, complete with games, music, and a photo booth. Employees enjoyed an afternoon of fun and bonding, reinforcing the strong team spirit that drives the sales region's success.

Celebrating Togetherness Across Locations

What made this Christmas celebration truly special was the unity and spirit shared across all three locations. Despite being in different cities, the Ligen Power® family came together to celebrate, share joy, and strengthen bonds. Each location brought its unique flavor to the celebrations while maintaining the core values of gratitude, innovation, and community service that define Ligen Power®.

Reflecting on the Year

As we celebrated Christmas, we also took time to reflect on the achievements and milestones of the past year. From technological innovations to community engagement, 2024 has been a year of growth and progress for Ligen Power®. The festive celebrations served as a perfect opportunity to acknowledge the hard work and dedication of our team members across all locations.

Looking Forward to 2025

As we bid farewell to 2024 and welcome 2025, Ligen Power® remains committed to innovation, sustainability, and community engagement. The Christmas celebrations have set a positive tone for the year ahead, filled with new opportunities, challenges, and achievements.
