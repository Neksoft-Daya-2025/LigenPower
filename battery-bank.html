The 25 kW class rack-mounted battery bank combines high capacity with a compact footprint: stacked modules, integrated control and monitoring, and casters for repositioning as your site evolves.

  • Save floor space — high power density in a vertical rack layout
  • Zero routine maintenance — sealed LiFePO4 chemistry
  • Integrated smart BMS — overcharge, over-discharge, overcurrent, thermal protection

  • Heavy-duty resilience — motors, compressors, and industrial loads

Why this rack matters

A 240 V DC, 100 Ah LFP tower—about 24 kWh nominal—built for sites that need dependable power, clear status at a glance, and room to scale.

  • LiFePO4 chemistry for thermal stability, long cycle life (3500+ cycles @ 80% DOD), and predictable runtime.
  • Integrated BMS with cell balancing, over/under-voltage, over-current, and temperature safeguards.
  • Industrial I/O—RS485 / CAN for monitoring and integration with inverters and site SCADA.
  • Parallel-ready—up to 20 units for larger banks; CE, UN38.3, MSDS aligned documentation for deployment confidence.
