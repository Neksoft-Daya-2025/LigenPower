Mr. T V Narendran Visits IIT Patna as New BoG Chairman

January 10, 2026 IIT Patna
Back to News & Events

Event Details

Date: January 9, 2026
Location: IIT Patna Campus
Guest: Mr. T V Narendran, Global CEO & MD, Tata Steel

Mr. T V Narendran, the newly appointed Chairman of the Board of Governors (BoG) of IIT Patna, visited the IIT Patna campus on Friday, January 9, 2026. He was appointed to this prestigious position by the Ministry of Education. Prof. T. N. Singh, Director, IIT Patna; Prof. A. K. Thakur, Dean Administration-cum-Registrar; and senior faculty members warmly received him at the Institute's helipad.

Mr. T V Narendran is the Global CEO and Managing Director of Tata Steel, bringing his extensive leadership experience to guide IIT Patna's future growth and development.

Comprehensive Institute Overview

The senior authorities of the Institute presented a comprehensive overview of Indian Institute of Technology, Patna's academic programmes, research and development activities, infrastructure expansion, new initiatives, placements, industry collaborations, and other key institutional developments to Mr. Narendran during a meeting held in the Institute's Board Room.

Distinguished Attendees

The meeting took place in the esteemed presence of the Director, Prof. T. N. Singh; the Dean Administration-cum-Registrar, Prof. A. K. Thakur; and several distinguished Deans, ADeans, HoDs, and faculty members, including:

  • Prof. Jimson Mathew
  • Prof. Rajiv Misra
  • Prof. Jawar Singh
  • Prof. Somanath Tripathy
  • Dr. N. K. Tomar
  • Dr. Anup K. Keshri
  • Dr. Subrata Hait
  • Dr. Sushant Kumar
  • Dr. Sweta Sinha
  • Dr. Dinesh Kumar Kotnees
  • Other faculty members of the Institute

Chairman's Appreciation

Mr. Narendran expressed his happiness and satisfaction with the institute's steady academic progress, research output, and infrastructure growth. His positive remarks reflect the institution's commitment to excellence and continuous improvement in all areas of academic and research endeavors.

Visit to Incubation Centre

Mr. Narendran also visited the Incubation Centre at IIT Patna, where he interacted with startup teams and learned about their ongoing projects and achievements. He commended the innovative work and the consistent progress made by the institute over the years across all fronts.

The Chairman's visit to the Incubation Centre highlights IIT Patna's commitment to fostering entrepreneurship and innovation among students and faculty members.

Token of Appreciation

Before the conclusion of his visit, Prof. T. N. Singh, Director, IIT Patna, presented a memento to Shri T. V. Narendran as a token of respect and appreciation for his new role and his visit to the campus.

About IIT Patna

Indian Institute of Technology Patna is one of India's premier technical institutions, known for its excellence in education, research, and innovation. The institute continues to make significant strides in various fields of engineering, science, and technology, contributing to the nation's development through its academic and research initiatives.
