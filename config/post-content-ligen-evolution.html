Why Electric Bicycles Are Ideal for Daily Commute in India

Eco-Friendly & Sustainable

With rising pollution levels in cities, electric bicycles provide zero tailpipe emissions, helping reduce carbon footprint while supporting India's sustainability goals under the National Electric Mobility Mission.

Perfect for Indian Traffic

Electric bicycles allow riders to bypass congested roads using service lanes and shortcuts, something petrol scooters often lack during peak hours.

Cost-Effective Daily Travel

With petrol prices consistently hovering between ₹100–110 per litre, daily commuting costs add up quickly. In comparison, electric bicycles typically cost ₹5–10 per full charge, helping riders save a significant amount every year.

No License Required

As per Indian RTO rules, electric bicycles with motor power up to 250W and top speed limited to 25 km/h do not require a driving license. All models in the LIGEN Evolution Series comply fully with these norms, making them suitable for students, professionals, and senior citizens.

Health Benefits Without Exhaustion

Pedal-assist technology encourages light physical activity without causing fatigue. This makes electric bicycles particularly suitable for people with sedentary routines, including professionals working in IT hubs like Bengaluru and Hyderabad.
