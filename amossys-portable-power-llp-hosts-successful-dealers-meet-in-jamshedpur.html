Amossys Portable Power LLP Hosts Successful Dealers' Meet in Jamshedpur

January 21, 2025 Jamshedpur
Back to News & Events

Event Details

Date: January 21, 2025
Location: Jamshedpur

Amossys Portable Power LLP, organized a dynamic and engaging Dealers' Meet on January 21, 2025, in Jamshedpur. The event was a remarkable platform for strengthening business collaborations, unveiling product innovations, and celebrating the trust and commitment shared between the company and its dealer network.

Showcasing Latest Ligen Power® Inverters

This event showcased the latest range of Ligen Power® inverters, renowned for their superior performance, durability, and cutting-edge design. The highlight of the showcase was the company's 6-year warranty offering, which emphasized Amossys's dedication to delivering long-lasting, reliable power solutions.

Open Forum for Ideas and Insights

The Dealers' Meet also served as an open forum for exchanging ideas and insights about market trends, growth opportunities, and strategies to drive success in the portable power solutions industry. Dealers actively participated in discussions, sharing valuable feedback that underscored their crucial role in Ligen Power®'s growth story.

Commitment to Innovation and Excellence

The event reinforced Amossys Portable Power LLP's commitment to innovation and excellence. By hosting such an interactive gathering, the company demonstrated its focus on not only providing industry-leading products but also fostering enduring relationships with its stakeholders.

Charting New Milestones

With the unwavering support of its trusted dealers and partners, Amossys is set to chart new milestones in the industry. The Dealers' Meet in Jamshedpur was not just a business event but a celebration of mutual success and shared vision for the future of portable power solutions.

6-Year Warranty: A Testament to Quality

The announcement of the 6-year warranty on Ligen Power® inverters was a significant highlight of the event. This warranty offering demonstrates Amossys's confidence in the quality and durability of its products, providing dealers and customers with unparalleled peace of mind and value.

Building Strong Dealer Relationships

The interactive nature of the Dealers' Meet allowed for meaningful conversations between the company leadership and its dealer network. These discussions covered various aspects of the business, from product features and market positioning to after-sales support and growth strategies.

Looking Ahead

Together, powering progress with trust and innovation. The successful Dealers' Meet in Jamshedpur has set a strong foundation for continued collaboration and growth. Amossys Portable Power LLP looks forward to building on this momentum and achieving new heights in the portable power solutions industry.
