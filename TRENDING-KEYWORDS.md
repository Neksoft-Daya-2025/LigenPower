## How to Use

1. **Titles & meta:** Use 1 primary keyword (e.g. “best electric bicycle for daily commute India”) + “Ligen Power®” in `<title>` and meta description.  
2. **H1/H2:** Use exact or close variants of keywords in headings.  
3. **Content:** Use 2–3 related keywords per section; avoid stuffing.  
4. **Blog:** Target long-tail and “best X for Y” with full articles (e.g. “Best Electric Bicycle for Daily Commute in India 2026” – you already have this).  
5. **Product pages:** Lead with product name + main keyword (e.g. “Ligen Power® 300 – 300VA Power Inverter for Home”).  
6. **Location:** Add city/state keywords on contact, test ride, and location-specific landing pages.  

**Tip:** Check **Google Trends** (trends.google.com) for “power inverter”, “electric cycle”, “solar inverter” in India to see which terms are rising and plan content/ads accordingly.
